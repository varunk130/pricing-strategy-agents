# Rollout planning

Move customers in waves so the team learns before the biggest accounts renew.

## Steps
1. Pull the renewal calendar under the draft model.
2. **Wave 1:** new customers plus renewals that save money or stay flat. This proves the pitch with low risk.
3. **Wave 2:** renewals with increases in the next two quarters. Send notice at least 120 days ahead and set up executive calls for the largest increases.
4. **Wave 3:** everyone else at their renewal date, with a review of overage rates after 6 months.

For each wave give a name, timing, scope (how many accounts and how much ARR) and 2 to 3 actions.

## Sales enablement
List 3 to 4 things the sales and success teams need, such as a one page pitch, a usage forecast for each account, approval rules for exceptions, and a comp plan change so reps are paid on usage commits.
