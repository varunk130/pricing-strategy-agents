# Usage based packaging for enterprise

Enterprise buyers want predictable spend. Vendors want revenue that grows with value. A hybrid model gives both.

- **Seat fee** for the people who build and manage work. This keeps accounts with low AI use stable.
- **Platform fee per tier** that includes a pool of usage. This gives the buyer a fixed budget line.
- **Overage per 1,000 units** that gets cheaper in higher tiers. This rewards commitment and creates an upgrade path.

## Rules of thumb
- Use 3 tiers. Size each included pool to cover the median account in that tier, so most customers see no overage in year 1.
- Lower the overage rate about 20% per tier.
- Seat price can drop 10% to 20% from today, because usage now carries the growth.
- Do not let a low usage account's price drop more than about 10%. That is lost revenue.
- Name tiers by customer stage, not size.
