# Competitive pricing teardown

For each competitor, note the model (seat, usage, hybrid or outcome), the unit, the entry price, the enterprise price, and what buyers complain about.

## What to look for
- **Direction of the market.** How many competitors have already moved off seats?
- **Buyer pain we can use.** Surprise invoices from pure usage. Paying twice for seats plus usage add ons. Outcome prices nobody can audit.
- **Price anchors.** Convert every price to cost per 1,000 units at enterprise volume, so the team knows the range buyers will compare against.
- **Do not** match the cheapest competitor. Pick a position (premium, parity or value) and give the reason.

Output 3 to 5 short insights, one positioning sentence, and a low and high anchor per 1,000 units.
