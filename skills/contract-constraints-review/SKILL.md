# Contract constraints review

A new price only works if existing contracts allow it. Before anyone designs tiers, find the limits.

## Check
- **Price protection clauses.** How many contracts cap renewal increases, and at what percent? How much ARR sits under a cap?
- **Renewal timing.** How many renewals land in each quarter? The first quarter is where the change is tested.
- **Multi year terms.** These cannot change until they renew.
- **Procurement patterns.** Fixed annual budgets, notice periods and approval rules shape how fast we can move.

## Output
- Constraints: facts the new model must respect, with numbers.
- Guardrails: rules the pricing team should adopt, for example a cap on year 1 increases or a notice period. Keep each to one sentence.
