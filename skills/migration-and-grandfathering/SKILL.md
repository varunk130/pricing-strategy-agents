# Migration and grandfathering

A pricing model is only as good as its migration plan. Judge every option on existing customers, not new ones.

## Guardrails to test
- **Year 1 increase cap.** Limit any customer's increase at renewal, usually 10% to 20%. Above 25%, churn risk rises fast.
- **Contract price caps.** Always honor caps already in contracts. Breaking them costs trust and invites legal review.
- **Keep negotiated discounts.** Changing the model and the discount in the same conversation doubles the fight.
- **Ramps.** Customers above the cap move to the new price over two renewals.

## How to tune
1. Simulate the model. Read retention against the seat model, ARR at risk, and the largest increases.
2. Change one lever at a time and simulate again. Write one line on what each change did.
3. Stop when ARR at risk is close to zero and retention is still clearly above the seat model. Run 4 simulations at most.
