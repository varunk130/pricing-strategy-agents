# Pricing decision memo

Readers: CRO, CFO and VP of Customer Success. They read the first three lines and skim the rest.

Use these sections in markdown:
1. **Decision requested.** One sentence.
2. **Why now.** The seat problem, with the retention number if nothing changes.
3. **Recommended model.** A table of tiers (platform fee, included runs, overage) plus the seat price.
4. **Impact on current customers.** Retention on seats vs the new model, year 1 ARR, how many save or pay more, and ARR at risk.
5. **Top risks.** Up to 4, each with its fix, from the red team.
6. **Rollout.** The 3 waves with timing.
7. **What we are not doing.** 2 options we rejected and why.

Use exact numbers. Plain words. No dashes used as punctuation. Under 400 words.
